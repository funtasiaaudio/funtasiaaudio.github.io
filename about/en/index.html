Creator · Founder

Hʉeliang

Composer / Music Producer
FUNtasia Founder

Born in Tokyo on 28 March 1996. Graduated from Senzoku Gakuen College of Music.

I started playing the piano at age three and began composing at sixteen.

My favourite music includes Vocaloid, visual kei, EDM, hardstyle, epic metal and neo-medieval music. I love combining different genres and turning sounds I imagine into something real.

I also develop software and plug-ins for music production.

With FUNtasia, I want to help create a world where anyone can enjoy making things, regardless of their financial circumstances or whether they have a disability.

I don't think music or software necessarily needs to reach a huge audience.

Rather than reaching lots of people a little, I would be happy to create something that deeply resonates with even one person.

That's the spirit in which I keep creating different things every day.

Whether you love music, software or simply want to try making something, you're always welcome to explore the world of FUNtasia.
