FUN MonoFilter

A free, lightweight VST3 audio plugin for controlling low-frequency stereo width.

Download ZIPPDF manual

What it does

Adjust the stereo width of low-frequency content in your mix.

Getting started

Download and extract the ZIP, then follow the included documentation for installation. Load FUN MonoFilter in your DAW and adjust while listening.
