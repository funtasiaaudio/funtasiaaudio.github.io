Newsletter Privacy Information

Information about subscribing to email updates from FUNtasia Audio.

How your email address is used

We use subscribed email addresses to send new product announcements, plugin updates, and important news, as well as to manage subscriptions and unsubscriptions.

Our email service

We use Brevo for subscription forms, mailing, and contact list management. Email addresses and subscription status submitted via the form are processed by Brevo. Our GitHub Pages site itself does not directly collect or store the email addresses entered into the form.

Confirming your subscription

Subscribing requires your consent and confirmation via a link sent to your email address. Your subscription is confirmed after you click that link.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in our newsletters. For questions about your newsletter email address, visit our official support page.
