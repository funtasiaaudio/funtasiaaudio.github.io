COLLABORATE

Collaborate with FUNtasia

FUNtasia Records welcomes messages from artists, vocalists, composers, musicians, visual and video creators, and others interested in creating with us.

We are still a small independent label, but our door is always open to people who connect with the music and world of FUNtasia and would like to create something together.

We are not looking for a particular genre. We are looking for music with a world of its own. Rather than fitting into a genre or trend, we value music that feels true to the person who created it.

Artists interested in releasing music through FUNtasia are also welcome to send us their demos.

If you are interested in working with us, please contact us by email with a brief introduction and links to your previous work.

OUR POLICY

AI & Creators' Rights

FUNtasia Records believes that exploring new creative technologies and respecting the rights and choices of every contributor go hand in hand.

How we use AI

We view AI as one of many tools that can support creative work. We may use it in music and visual production, planning and research, and the development of music-making software associated with FUNtasia. Using AI is not, in itself, the same as infringing another person's rights.

Handling contributed materials

Vocal recordings, voice data, performances, and other materials provided by collaborators are used only for the purposes and within the scope agreed upon in advance. We do not repurpose them without authorization.

AI training and voice imitation

We do not use a collaborator's supplied vocal or voice data to train AI models, imitate a particular voice, or reproduce or generate voice audio without that person's explicit permission.

Agreement and mutual respect

We respect the creative expression, rights, and choices of everyone involved. We strive to clarify intended uses and rights before work begins. Specific terms are governed by the agreement or contract for each project.
