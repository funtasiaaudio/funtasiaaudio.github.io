COLLABORATE

Collaborate with FUNtasia

FUNtasia Records welcomes messages from artists, vocalists, composers, musicians, visual and video creators, and others interested in creating with us.

We are still a small independent label, but our door is always open to people who connect with the music and world of FUNtasia and would like to create something together.

We are not looking for a particular genre. We are looking for music with a world of its own. Rather than fitting into a genre or trend, we value music that feels true to the person who created it.

Artists interested in releasing music through FUNtasia are also welcome to send us their demos.

If you are interested in working with us, please contact us by email with a brief introduction and links to your previous work.

Q&A

Music submissions and releases

Can I release music made with AI?

Yes. We treat music made with AI the same way as music made entirely by people. We place great importance on the world behind a piece of music. For songs with lyrics, share their meaning or story. For instrumental music, tell us about its setting or imagery. A short supporting document can help. If a track has simply been generated and we cannot see the creative intent behind it, it is unlikely to be accepted.

What kind of music is more likely to be accepted?

To be frank, music connected to our fictional world, ARCANA REVELIA, is more likely to be accepted. But you do not have to write for our world. Send us music that expresses your own, whatever the genre. We look forward to discovering unfamiliar worlds.

Visit the official FUNtasia World website to explore ARCANA REVELIA.

Are there any genre restrictions?

No. We welcome electronic music, classical, metal, folk, ambient, experimental music and work that crosses genre boundaries. What your music expresses matters more than its genre.

Can I submit instrumental music without lyrics?

Of course. Music can convey stories, feelings and landscapes without words. If there is a story, image or scene behind your piece, please share it with us.

Do I need an elaborate story or detailed worldbuilding?

Not necessarily. A landscape, a fleeting feeling, a personal memory or a fictional character's emotions can all form a world of their own. What matters is the thought and creative intent behind the music, not the scale of the setting or the length of the description.

Does music made without AI have an advantage?

No. We do not judge a piece by its production method alone. Its musicality, expression, creativity and world matter more than the instruments, software, techniques or use of AI.

Do I have to fit the FUNtasia Records world?

No. We are glad when our world resonates with artists, but we respect their individuality and worlds of their own. You do not need to change your artistic expression to fit our label.

Do popularity or past achievements affect selection?

We do not believe that popularity or experience alone determines the value of music. We want to listen to work by established and unknown artists alike as music in its own right.

If my music is not accepted, does that mean it is not good enough?

Not necessarily. Selection depends on several factors, including the label's direction and how well the work fits. Our decision does not diminish the value of your music.

What matters most to FUNtasia Records?

Discovering a world through music. There are stories, emotions, landscapes and the imagination of the person who created it. We believe music can be more than a collection of sounds: it can be an entrance to a world we have yet to discover.

OUR POLICY

AI & Creators' Rights

FUNtasia Records believes that exploring new creative technologies and respecting the rights and choices of every contributor go hand in hand.

How we use AI

We view AI as one of many tools that can support creative work. We may use it in music and visual production, planning and research, and the development of music-making software associated with FUNtasia. Using AI is not, in itself, the same as infringing another person's rights.

Handling contributed materials

Vocal recordings, voice data, performances, and other materials provided by collaborators are used only for the purposes and within the scope agreed upon in advance. We do not repurpose them without authorization.

AI training and voice imitation

We do not use a collaborator's supplied vocal or voice data to train AI models, imitate a particular voice, or reproduce or generate voice audio without that person's explicit permission.

Agreement and mutual respect

We respect the creative expression, rights, and choices of everyone involved. We strive to clarify intended uses and rights before work begins. Specific terms are governed by the agreement or contract for each project.
