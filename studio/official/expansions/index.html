02 / GET CREATIVE

A simple workflow

Make every sound your own.

  1. WAV packs (Orchestral Percussion / Nyan 8bit):Extract the ZIP and choose the recording or one-shot you want.
  2. Load the WAV into a sampler or audio track in FUNtasia Studio or another DAW. Arrange and layer sounds to make beats and game-style effects.
  3. MIDI packs (Epic Metal / Fantasy Folk):Extract the ZIP and open the MIDI folder.
  4. Add a GM-compatible drum instrument to an instrument track, then drag or import a .mid file. Edit notes and velocities to make it yours.
  5. Use fills to replace part of a groove: the final one or two bars for Epic Metal, or bar four for Fantasy Folk.
  6. Wavetable packs (Kawaii Wavetable):Extract the ZIP, import a wavetable WAV into The Wavelace, and explore morphing with WT POSITION.
